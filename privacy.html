LensDraft – Scanner & Resizer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026
On-Device Processing • Local Storage

LensDraft (“the App”) is a document scanner, passport photo creator, and photo/signature resizer developed and published by GharMantra Apps.

1. Summary

  • The App does not require an account or registration.
  • Scanning, cropping, OCR, background removal, PDF tools, and photo resizing run locally on your device.
  • We do not upload your documents, photos, or scans to our servers.
  • The App shows ads (Google AdMob) and offers subscriptions (Google Play Billing). These services use the internet and process certain technical data as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

Your scanned documents, PDFs, photos, and settings are stored strictly in the App’s local storage on your phone. We do not have access to them. Deleting the App removes this local data.

Backup is disabled for the App (android:allowBackup="false"), so your documents and scanned files are not included in Android cloud backups.

3. Information collected by third-party services

a) Google AdMob (advertising)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display ads to free users. The SDK may automatically collect and share with Google: your IP address (used to estimate general location), app interactions (such as taps and views), diagnostic information (such as crash logs and performance data), and device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. This data is encrypted in transit. We do not receive or access your documents through this process.

Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads; menu names vary by device).

b) Google Play Billing (subscriptions)

Payments and subscriptions are securely processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your credit card, debit card, or bank account details.

c) Google ML Kit (on-device features)

Features such as document scanning, text recognition (OCR), face detection, background removal, and QR scanning use Google ML Kit. These features process images on your device. ML Kit models are delivered through Google Play services, so they may be downloaded when you first use a feature or when the App is installed. This download needs an internet connection.

Face detection and background removal are used only to centre, crop, and edit your photo on your device. We do not create, store, or send face templates. Parents or guardians may prepare a child’s photo using the App; the photo stays on the device.

4. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, precise location, microphone audio, SMS, or call logs. We do not run any proprietary analytics, trackers, or crash-reporting services in the App.

5. Permissions

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): to load ads and verify subscriptions via Google Play.
  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): to scan documents, ID cards, and QR codes.
  • Photos/Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE with maxSdkVersion="32"): to pick images from your gallery and to save exported documents on older Android versions. (Android 13+ uses the system Photo Picker, requiring no storage permissions).
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to send optional, periodic feature discovery tips. You can turn them off anytime in Settings > Feature Tips & Periodic Reminders.
  • Run at startup (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to restore scheduled feature tips after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): to provide haptic feedback when a QR code is scanned.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.
  • Network state (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to check whether an internet connection is available for ads and subscriptions.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): to process optional LensDraft Pro subscriptions through Google Play.
  • Wake lock (android.permission.WAKE_LOCK): used by system components, such as scheduled notifications, to finish short background tasks reliably.

6. Subscriptions

LensDraft Pro removes all ads. Prices are ₹20.00 per month and ₹50.00 per 3 months (taxes and regional pricing may apply). Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. To avoid the next charge, cancel before the renewal date. Refunds follow Google Play’s policies.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and above and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18.

8. Your choices

  • Delete the App to permanently remove all local documents.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android device settings.
  • Turn off feature tips and notifications in the App’s Settings.
  • Contact us using the details in Section 12 for any privacy questions.

9. Data security

All document processing occurs locally on your device. Network data exchanged with Google’s advertising and billing services is encrypted in transit using TLS.

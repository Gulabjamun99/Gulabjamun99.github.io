LensDraft Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026
On-Device Processing • Local File Storage

LensDraft ("the App") is a document scanner, passport photo creator, and photo/signature resizer. It is developed and published by GharMantra Apps.

1. SUMMARY

  • No Account Required: The App does not require registration, login, or personal profile creation.
  • On-Device Processing: Scanning, cropping, OCR (optical character recognition), background removal, PDF utilities, and photo resizing run locally on your device.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: We do not upload your documents, photos, or scans to our servers.
  • Third-Party Services: The App displays advertisements (Google AdMob) and provides optional subscriptions (Google Play Billing). These services use the internet and may process certain non-identifying data, described below.

2. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Your scanned documents, PDFs, photos, and app settings are stored strictly in the App's local storage sandbox on your device. We do not have access to them.

Backup Disabled: System cloud backup is explicitly disabled for the App (android:allowBackup="false"), ensuring that your scanned documents and private files are never uploaded into automated Google Drive cloud backups. Deleting or uninstalling the App completely deletes this local data.

3. INFORMATION COLLECTED BY THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

a) Google AdMob (Advertising)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve advertisements to free-tier users. The SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AD_ID).
  • Approximate location: General coarse location derived from IP address (e.g., city or country level).
  • Performance & Diagnostics: Crash logs, app responsiveness, and ad interaction metrics.

b) Google Play Billing (Subscriptions)

The App offers an optional "LensDraft Pro" ad-free subscription processed securely through Google Play Billing. Google handles all payment and credit card transactions directly. We never receive or store your financial or payment details.

c) Google ML Kit (On-Device Machine Learning)

The App uses Google ML Kit libraries for on-device document scanning, text recognition (OCR), and face detection. All processing runs entirely on your device without sending image data to Google servers.

4. DATA NOT COLLECTED

We do NOT collect, read, or share:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • Your scanned documents, ID cards, government cards, or certificates.
  • Your photos, signature images, or generated PDFs.
  • Biometric data, precise GPS location, or device microphone audio.

5. PERMISSIONS USED BY THE APP

The App requests only permissions necessary to deliver its core utilities:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required to capture documents, ID cards, and portraits for passport photos.
  • Storage (Android 12 and below only): READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE with maxSdkVersion="32" are used solely on legacy Android devices to import and save documents. On Android 13+, the secure system Photo Picker is used, requiring zero storage permissions.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional, used to deliver periodic educational feature discovery tips. Can be toggled on/off at any time in Settings.
  • Network state (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): To check whether an internet connection is available for ads and subscriptions.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): To process optional LensDraft Pro subscriptions securely through Google Play.
  • Wake lock (android.permission.WAKE_LOCK): Used by system components (such as scheduled notifications and Google Play background receipt verification) to complete short background tasks reliably while the screen is off.

6. SUBSCRIPTIONS & CANCELLATION

Subscriptions are completely optional. All core scanning, ID card, OCR, and photo resizing features are 100% free to use with non-disruptive ads.

  • Plans: Monthly (₹20.00 / month) or Quarterly (₹50.00 / 3 months).
  • Auto-Renewal: Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  • Cancellation: To avoid the next charge, cancel before the renewal date. Refunds follow Google Play refund policies.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Because we do not operate user accounts or external servers, we hold no user data. You can delete all locally generated files and caches at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App from your device.
